Frequency fingerprint

High-pass spectrum, origin centred. Generators leave a lattice of bright periodic peaks; a camera leaves a smooth falloff. The number is how far the brightest peaks sit above the spread — lower is more photographic.

Simulates capture optics and sensor response. It does not remove C2PA provenance manifests or embedded watermarks, and writes no EXIF.
